Suppliers are the backbone of Pocket Network. They run the infrastructure that serves the network's data services, from blockchain RPC to REST APIs, LLM inference, and MCP servers, and earn POKT rewards for every relay they prove they delivered.

## What Suppliers Do

A Supplier is any entity that stakes POKT and runs a [RelayMiner](/node-operators/relayminer-setup) to serve requests for the services it is staked on. When an Application sends a relay (an API request to a service), the protocol assigns it to a session of Suppliers who compete to serve it. Suppliers that successfully serve relays and submit valid proofs earn POKT rewards.

## Why It Matters

Pocket Network's value proposition — decentralized, redundant access to data services, paid per request — depends entirely on Suppliers. More Suppliers means more geographic diversity, more service coverage, and more resilience. Serving the services that AI agents call through the [Agentic Portal](/agent-services) is a growing share of that work. The protocol's economics are designed to make running Supplier infrastructure profitable for operators who maintain high quality of service.
