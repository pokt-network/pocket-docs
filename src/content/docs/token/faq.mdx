## Getting and Holding POKT

**Where can I buy POKT?**
POKT trades on several centralized exchanges (see [Exchanges](/token/exchanges) for the current list). For decentralized trading, wPOKT is available on Uniswap, Aerodrome, Jupiter, and other DEXs. See the full [How to Buy](/token/how-to-buy) guide.

**What wallet do I use?**
[Soothe Vault](https://trustsoothe.io) is recommended for most holders — it supports both native POKT and wPOKT in one interface. [Keplr](https://www.keplr.app/) and [Leap](https://leapwallet.io) work for native POKT as Cosmos ecosystem wallets. See [Wallets](/wallets/).

**What is wPOKT?**
An ERC-20/SPL version of POKT bridged to Ethereum, Base, and Solana. You don't need wPOKT if you plan to hold or stake on Pocket mainnet. It's useful for DEX trading, DeFi liquidity provision, or holding in an EVM wallet. You can [bridge](/token/bridge) between native POKT and wPOKT.

**What is POKT's address format?**
Shannon uses Cosmos bech32 format: addresses start with `pokt1` followed by lowercase letters and numbers. This is different from Morse-era addresses.

**I had POKT in a Morse wallet — did I lose it?**
No. Shannon stores a complete archive of all Morse balances. You need to claim your Morse account on Shannon by following the migration instructions. If your POKT was on an exchange or with a noderunner, they handled it on your behalf. See [Shannon Upgrade](/deprecated/shannon-upgrade).

## Tokenomics and Supply

**Is POKT inflationary or deflationary?**
Deflationary since [PIP-41](/token/pip-41) (February 2026). For every relay served, all of the POKT fee is burned but only <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" format="percent" /> is re-minted. The remaining <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" complement format="percent" /> is permanently removed. More relay volume = more deflation. This is automatic — no governance vote needed per burn.

**What is the total supply?**
About <NetworkStat metric="total_supply" format="billions" fallback="see Pocket Analytics" /> POKT, and declining. There is no fixed maximum supply cap, but under PIP-41, supply contracts with every relay served.

**How does burn-and-mint work?**
Applications burn POKT to pay for relays. The protocol mints <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" format="percent" /> back and distributes it: <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.supplier" format="percent" /> to Suppliers, <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.proposer" format="percent" /> to Validators, <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.dao" format="percent" /> to the DAO, and <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.source_owner" format="percent" /> to service owners. The remaining <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" complement format="percent" /> of the original burn is never created. See [Tokenomics](/token/tokenomics) for the full model.

**What is the CUTTM?**
The Compute Unit to Token Multiplier — the exchange rate converting compute units to POKT. Since relay pricing is pegged to USD (1 billion CUs ≈ $1), the DAO adjusts CUTTM as POKT's price changes to keep dollar costs stable. A lower POKT price → higher CUTTM → more POKT burned per relay → faster deflation (natural price support).

## Staking

**Can I earn yield?**
Yes, by running a Supplier node or delegating stake. Suppliers earn <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.supplier" format="percent" /> of minted POKT from every relay they serve. There is no fixed APY — earnings are proportional to work served. See [Staking](/token/staking).

**What are my staking options?**
Run your own node (<GovParam module="supplier" param="min_stake" format="pokt" /> POKT minimum + server infrastructure), delegate to a noderunner (they run infrastructure, you provide stake), or join a staking pool (smaller amounts, pooled model). See [Staking](/token/staking) for details on each.

## Governance

**Can I vote?**
Yes, through the CREDS governance system. The DAO has two Houses: Builder House (80% weight, one-person-one-vote for verified contributors) and Staker House (20%, stake-weighted). Voting happens on [Snapshot](https://snapshot.org/#/pokt-dao.eth). See [Governance](/governance/).

**What does the DAO control?**
All key protocol parameters (relay pricing, mint ratio, reward splits, staking requirements) plus the DAO treasury (funded by <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.dao" format="percent" /> of all minted POKT).

## The Network

**What is Shannon?**
The codename for Pocket Network's current protocol — a full Cosmos SDK rebuild launched June 2025. It replaced Morse with permissionless gateways, usage-based pricing, burn-and-mint tokenomics, and support for any open data service. Named after Claude Shannon, the founder of information theory.

**What chains does Pocket support?**
Dozens of blockchain networks and L2s. The public RPC list is on [Supported Chains](/developers/supported-chains), and every registered service is at [explorer.pocket.network/services](https://explorer.pocket.network/services). New chains can be added permissionlessly under Shannon.

**Is Pocket just for blockchain data?**
No. Pocket serves any HTTP data service: data APIs, LLM inference, document tools, MCP servers, and blockchain RPC. Most new services are built for AI agents, which find them and pay per call on the [Agentic Portal](/agent-services). To publish your own, see [Services](/services/).
