POKT is the native token of Pocket Network. It's not just a governance token or speculative asset — it's the fuel that runs a live, global data network. Every relay served burns POKT. Every node operator earns POKT.

## Supply at a Glance

- **Total supply:** <NetworkStat metric="total_supply" format="billions" fallback="see live figures below" /> POKT, declining under [PIP-41](/token/pip-41) deflation
- **Burn vs. mint:** At current relay volume, burn roughly equals mint — net deflationary. Live daily burn/mint and staked-supply figures: [pokt.money](https://pokt.money) and [Pocket Analytics](https://analytics.pocket.network)
- **Genesis vesting:** Fully complete — no remaining cliff or vesting unlocks

The total supply above is fetched live from the chain on every build. For real-time market data (price, market cap), see [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/coins/pocket-network).

## The Token Flow

1. **Applications** burn POKT to pay for data access (relay fees)
2. **The protocol** mints <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" format="percent" /> of what was burned ([PIP-41](/token/pip-41) removes the other <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_ratio" complement format="percent" />)
3. **Suppliers** receive <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.supplier" format="percent" /> of minted tokens for serving relays
4. **Validators, DAO, and Source Owners** split the remaining <GovParam module="tokenomics" param="mint_equals_burn_claim_distribution.supplier" complement format="percent" />

More usage → more burn → less supply → increased scarcity.
